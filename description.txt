[h1]Description[/h1]

HUI: Helpful Utility Items is a mod that adds small changes to the game to improve the quality of life.
The mod requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2824688072]Calamity[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2831360228]Project tRU[/url] (for Russians).
Currently there are 37 items, 6 effects, 3 NPCs and 2 tiles in the mod.
